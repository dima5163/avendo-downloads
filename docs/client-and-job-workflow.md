# A first client-and-job workflow for freelancers

Avendo is a lightweight business assistant for freelancers and small service
businesses. It brings clients, jobs, payment status, tasks, reminders and bookings
into one workspace. This guide suggests a small working routine; it does not
require moving all of your work at once.

## 1. Start with the client you need next

Choose an app from the [official download page](https://avendo-app.com/en/download/).
The free local organizer works offline on mobile and desktop without an account.
Cloud sync, web access and other optional features depend on the plan you choose;
check the [current plans](https://avendo-app.com/en/pricing/) before relying on them.

Begin with one real client and the work you are currently doing for them. Keep
client information with the related jobs rather than scattering it across a
separate list of notes. Add more clients when the first workflow is useful.

## 2. Separate jobs from next actions

Use jobs to keep track of the work you have agreed to do. Use tasks and reminders
for the next actions needed to move that work forward. For example, agreeing on
a piece of work and remembering to follow up are different things to track.

At the start of a working day, review the active work and identify the next
useful action. Keep the routine short enough that you can repeat it tomorrow.

## 3. Keep payment status current

Track the payment status of your jobs alongside the work. Check it against your
actual payment records when you update it. Update the status from what has actually happened, rather than treating a
reminder as a payment record.

## 4. Add bookings when they fit your work

If clients book time with you, review Avendo's booking features and the plan
requirements. The optional Pro plan includes a booking page. You can also use
the client-and-job routine without making a public booking page part of it.

## 5. Review before adding more

After using the routine on real work, check whether your client information,
active jobs, payment statuses and next actions are up to date. Add more work
only when you can keep that information useful.

For installation, return to the [download instructions](../README.md#installing).
Release binaries are available through the [official releases](https://github.com/dima5163/avendo-downloads/releases).
